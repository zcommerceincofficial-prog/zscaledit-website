How it works

  2. 02

Say a shop signs on at $1,500 a month. That's $300 a month to you, or $900 over 3 months.

The fine print

  • You get 20% of the monthly fee they pay me. Their ad spend doesn't count. That goes straight to Google and Facebook.
  • You're paid each month for their first 3 paid months, after their payment clears. If they cancel early, the payments stop.
  • The shop has to be new to me. If we're already talking, it doesn't count.
  • If two people send the same shop, the first form in gets the credit.
  • I work with one shop per service in each market. If their market is taken, I can't take them on.
